Reproducibility finding:
The uploaded intermediate load_profile_hourly.csv, when scaled using the
uploaded feeder formula (gamma 1.20, target peak 1.75 MW), does not reproduce
the exact total_load_kW stochastic realization in the optimizer CSV. The
seasonal/model shape is consistent, but the hourly stochastic realization is
different. Therefore the package retains the exact optimizer load CSV as the
authoritative reference and audits any fresh model rebuild before replacement.
This prevents optimizer results from being silently paired with a new random
load year.
