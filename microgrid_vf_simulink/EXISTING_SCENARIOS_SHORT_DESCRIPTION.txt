SHORT DESCRIPTION OF THE EXISTING MICROGRID V/f SCENARIOS
==========================================================

The package runs five presentation simulations. Scenario IDs 31 and 32 are
named 3A and 3B, so there are five simulations even though the internal IDs
are 1, 2, 31, 32, and 4.


SCENARIO 1 - GRID LOSS AND ISLANDING
Internal ID: 1
Simulation time: 6 s
Event: Grid/PCC loss at 2.0 s; non-critical load is shed at 2.1 s.

Before the outage, the grid, PV, and BESS supply the feeder and diesel is
OFF. After grid loss, the BESS becomes the grid-forming source. The
non-critical load is disconnected 100 ms later and the surviving island
serves the critical load using PV + BESS. This case demonstrates successful
transition from grid-connected to islanded operation and load shedding.


SCENARIO 2 - +20% CRITICAL-LOAD STEP
Internal ID: 2
Simulation time: 6 s
Event: Critical load increases by 20% at 2.0 s.

The system is already islanded with PV + grid-forming BESS; grid and diesel
are OFF and non-critical load is disconnected. The sudden load increase
creates an active-power imbalance and the BESS responds through its droop
and dispatch dynamics. This case demonstrates frequency/voltage recovery
following a load disturbance that is still within BESS capability.


SCENARIO 3A - 50% PV AVAILABILITY DROP
Internal ID: 31
Simulation time: 10 s
Event: Available PV power falls by 50% at 2.0 s.

The system is islanded with PV + grid-forming BESS and no diesel. The code
automatically selects a real operating hour where PV initially exceeds the
critical load, so the BESS is charging before the disturbance. After the PV
drop, the BESS changes toward discharge and covers the new deficit. This
case demonstrates renewable-power disturbance rejection and BESS support.


SCENARIO 3B - DIESEL TRIP / INSUFFICIENT CAPACITY
Internal ID: 32
Simulation time: 4 s
Event: Diesel generator trips at 2.0 s.

The island is initially supplied by PV + grid-forming BESS + diesel because
PV and BESS alone are not sufficient for the selected critical-load point.
When diesel trips, the remaining PV + BESS capacity cannot fully supply the
load. The resulting sustained power deficit causes frequency/voltage to
fail to recover normally. This is intentionally the physical-limit case and
shows that control cannot compensate for insufficient generation capacity.


SCENARIO 4 - GRID RESTORATION AND RESYNCHRONIZATION
Internal ID: 4
Simulation time: 8 s
Event: Utility grid becomes available again at 2.0 s, but the PCC remains
open until synchronization requirements are met.

The microgrid starts islanded with PV + grid-forming BESS. An initial phase
error of 15 degrees is imposed as a synchronization stress condition. After
the grid returns, the controller corrects the island frequency/phase. The
PCC closes only after phase, frequency, and voltage errors remain inside the
specified synchronization window for the required hold time. This case
demonstrates controlled reconnection rather than immediate breaker closure.


COMMON MODEL NOTES
------------------
- Equivalent bus: 400 V line-line, 50 Hz.
- Grid/PV/BESS/diesel sizes come from the optimizer result file.
- BESS is the grid-forming source during islanded operation.
- PV is represented as grid-following active-power generation.
- Positive BESS power = discharge; negative BESS power = charge.
- Positive grid power = import; negative grid power = export.
- The model is reduced-order/average-value and is intended for V/f and
  power-sharing demonstrations, not detailed EMT fault/CCT analysis.
